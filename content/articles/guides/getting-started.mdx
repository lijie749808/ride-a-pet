Welcome! This guide walks you through everything you need to know on your very first day.

## Your Starting Tools

When you arrive at your farm you'll have these basic tools in your bag:

- **Watering Can** — Waters one crop tile per use
- **Hoe** — Tills soil so you can plant seeds
- **Axe** — Clears stumps and trees for wood
- **Pickaxe** — Breaks rocks and boulders
- **Sword** — Basic combat weapon

## Setting Up Your Farm

Start by clearing land. Chop trees with your **Axe** to collect Wood and use the **Pickaxe** to remove rocks — both are needed for crafting.

### Planting Your First Crops

1. Equip the **Hoe** and click the ground to till soil
2. Open your inventory and select seeds
3. Place seeds in tilled tiles
4. Water them with your **Watering Can** every morning

> **Tip:** Crops that aren't watered for a full day won't advance their growth timer.

## Energy Management

Every tool use costs energy. Watch the bar at the bottom of the screen — when empty you'll move slowly and eventually collapse and wake up in bed.

Restore energy by eating foraged food or cooked meals.

## Next Steps

Once you're settled, focus on these priorities:

1. Upgrade tools at the Blacksmith
2. Explore the Mine for ore
3. Build friendships with villagers
4. Unlock the **Sprinkler** to automate watering

Good luck on your adventure!
