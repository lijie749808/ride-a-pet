Whether you're a new farmer or a seasoned veteran, these tips will help you maximize your farm's potential.

## Sprinkler Placement Strategy

The **Sprinkler** is one of the most valuable items in the game. Each one waters a 3×3 area around itself.

For maximum coverage with zero overlap, space them out like this:

```
. . . . . . .
. S . . . S .
. . . . . . .
. . . . . . .
. S . . . S .
. . . . . . .
```

> **Pro Tip:** Upgrade to the Quality Sprinkler (5×5 coverage) as soon as you can!

## Seasonal Planning

Each season is exactly 28 days. Plan around multi-harvest crops for maximum profit.

| Season | Days | Best Crops |
|--------|------|------------|
| Spring | 28 | Strawberry, Cauliflower |
| Summer | 28 | Blueberry, Melon |
| Fall | 28 | Cranberry, Pumpkin |
| Winter | 28 | Indoor crops only |

## Crop Value Ranking

Focus your limited watering energy on the highest-value crops.

**Spring picks:**
1. **Strawberry** — Re-harvests every 4 days after first yield
2. **Rhubarb** — High single-harvest value from the Desert shop

**Summer picks:**
1. **Blueberry** — Re-harvests every 4 days, drops 3 berries each time
2. **Hops** — Excellent for artisan goods (Pale Ale)

## Fertilizer Guide

Apply fertilizer **before planting** for the best results.

| Fertilizer | Quality Boost | How to Craft |
|------------|---------------|--------------|
| Basic Fertilizer | +1 tier chance | 2 Sap |
| Quality Fertilizer | +2 tier chance | 2 Sap + 1 Fish |
| Deluxe Fertilizer | Max quality | Gained from Qi Gems |

## Energy-Efficient Farming

- Plant crops in compact clusters to minimize walking distance
- Water high-value crops first in case you run out of energy
- Use Sprinklers to eliminate daily watering entirely

Happy farming!
